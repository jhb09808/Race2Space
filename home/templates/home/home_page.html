About the project
Which developing countries have space agencies? Collapse
Race2Space profiles {{ num_agencies }} developing-country space agencies across Africa, Asia and Latin America — including India (ISRO), Brazil (AEB), Argentina (CONAE), Egypt (EgSA), South Africa (SANSA), Kenya (KENSA), Pakistan (SUPARCO), Indonesia, the Philippines (PhilSA), Türkiye, Iran, Azerbaijan and Ukraine.
How much do they spend on space? Expand
Annual budgets range from around $220 million for the largest spenders such as Azerbaijan and Indonesia, down to a few million for emerging programs. Compare them on the live dashboard.
Which nation has launched the most objects? Expand
Among developing nations, Brazil leads with around 42 objects launched, followed by Türkiye (~32), Indonesia (~23) and Argentina (~19).
Have they reached the Moon or Mars? Expand
Yes. India reached Mars orbit on its first attempt in 2014 and landed near the Moon's south pole with Chandrayaan-3 in 2023. The UAE's Hope probe reached Mars in 2021.
Is the data really live? Expand
Launches, satellite positions and crew-in-space update in real time from open feeds; agency budgets are the latest annual figures.
